Control noise distribution (step 0 of horizon)

Drag the goal, start or obstacles. Double-click empty space to add an obstacle, double-click an obstacle to remove it. Shift+double-click empty space adds a movable (red) object; shift+double-click an obstacle toggles it between fixed and movable. The rover pushes red objects out of its way. Drag the timeline to inspect past timesteps; pressing Play from there resumes from that step. Space = play/pause, ←/→ = step.
